## 2025-05-15 - [Skip to Content in Documentation]
**Learning:** Documentation generators with a sticky or long sidebar navigation significantly hinder keyboard and screen reader accessibility if they lack a "Skip to Content" link. Users are forced to navigate through the entire link list on every page before reaching the main content.
**Action:** Always implement a "Skip to Content" link as the first child of the body, targeting the main content area with proper focus management (id and tabindex).
