## 2025-05-15 - [A11y & Navigation Improvements for Documentation]
**Learning:** Skip-to-content links and ARIA current markers are essential for keyboard and screen reader accessibility in long-form documentation. Proper focus management via `tabindex="-1"` on the main content target ensures smooth navigation after skipping.
**Action:** Always include ARIA landmarks and focus management when generating multi-page documentation or complex layouts to provide a predictable and accessible experience for all users.
